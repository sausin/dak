Dak is an open-source SMS and MMS app for the messages people actually rely on: one-time passwords, bank alerts, tickets and deliveries. It sorts them for you, keeps them safe from scams, and never uploads them. There are no ads, no analytics and no account.

Everything runs on your phone. The app works fully offline; the only network use is your carrier's own MMS download.

ORGANISED AUTOMATICALLY
• Tabs for Personal, Transactions, OTP, Promotions and Spam, sorted on the device
• Bank and business senders folded into one thread per brand
• Powerful search: from:, amount:>500, has:otp, before:, during:"last week" and more
• Tappable phone numbers, amounts, UPI IDs, PNRs and tracking numbers

OTPS, DONE RIGHT
• Big, clear OTP notification with the code copied on arrival
• Codes your banking app has already read go quiet and delete themselves

PASSBOOK FROM YOUR SMS
• Transactions grouped by bank account, card, wallet, UPI, loan, mutual fund and demat account
• Every amount kept in its original currency; balances never invented
• Messages flagged as likely scams never reach the ledger

SCAM AND FRAUD DEFENCE
• Detects fake "money credited" messages, "sent by mistake, please return" tricks and UPI collect bait
• Warns about look-alike links, spoofed sender names and hidden characters
• Links always need a second tap
• One-tap fraud reporting with details pre-filled (for example 1930 and 1909 in India)

PRIVACY
• Incognito chats that delete your copy after reading or sending
• App lock with biometrics or a PIN, and "hide in Recents"
• Encrypted local index; export or delete all of Dak's data from Settings

RELIABLE
• Every incoming message is written to the phone's own SMS store first, so OTP autofill in other apps keeps working
• Notification self-test and guidance for phones that kill background apps
• Dual SIM, group MMS, delivery ticks, scheduled sends with a heads-up before they go out

AUTOMATIONS WITH GUARDRAILS
• Time-limited auto-forwarding to saved contacts, protected by app lock and biometric confirmation
• Birthday and anniversary wishes, "ask first" or automatic
• Broadcast lists with spam-risk checks and daily limits

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• End-to-end encrypted backups to a folder or cloud storage you choose
• Open, documented export format
• Import from SMS Backup & Restore and other common backup files

Dak needs to be your default SMS app to read, send and store messages. It asks for SMS permissions only after you choose it as the default.

Made for India first, and usable anywhere. Source code (MIT licence): https://github.com/sausin/dak
